## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines smart retail POS and business management for MSMEs through a warm, human-centric aesthetic that fuses high-touch hospitality with operational precision. Instead of cold, clinical gray SaaS canvases or aggressive neon fintech visuals, the interface is rooted in "Warm Minimalist Claymorphism." It combines tactile, soft-extruded porcelain forms, velvety terracotta accents, warm caramel tones, and deeply cushioned ambient drop shadows.

The experience is crafted for dual audiences: fast-moving cashiers on countertop tablets or mobile handsets needing instant visual feedback, and boutique business owners monitoring margins and inventory. The visual language conveys warmth, hospitality, organic reliability, and reassuring calm.

### Design Movement: Tactile Claymorphism & Organic Warmth
- **Physicality without Skeuomorphism:** Elements appear carved from smooth porcelain, matte ceramic, and warm clay. Surfaces feature gentle ambient multi-directional shadows and soft internal bevels rather than dated metallic gradients or heavy plastic gloss.
- **Human-Centric Comfort:** Off-white porcelain backdrops (`#FAF5EE` and `#F8F3EA`) eliminate cashier eye fatigue under fluorescent counter lighting.
- **Brand Dignity:** Rooted in the official RASH emblem—intertwining refined typography with geometric mandalas—contrasting terracotta warmth with signature deep teal and caramel gold.

## Colors

The palette establishes an appetizing, artisanal warmth centered on burnt terracotta, caramel, and oat milk porcelain, grounded by brand deep emerald teal.

### Primary, Secondary & Tertiary Distribution
- **Primary (`#B84E29` - Burnt Terracotta Orange):** The dominant action color. Applied to critical conversion triggers: "Order Now", primary POS checkout buttons, active bottom navigation pills, and dynamic product add buttons.
- **Secondary (`#D9822B` - Warm Caramel Gold):** Secondary actions, rewards and loyalty moments, active filters, and highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#0A4D4D` - Deep Emerald Teal):** The foundational RASH brand identity anchor. Reserved for the official emblem, system navigation headers, merchant identity locks, and operational verification badges.
- **Neutral (`#FAF5EE` - Oat Milk Porcelain):** The canvas background. Provides a warm, creamy alternative to sterile `#FFFFFF`, drastically softening glare on POS terminals.

### Neutral & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF5EE` / `#F8F3EA`):** Screen backgrounds, viewport frames, and empty state canvases.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** High-touch interaction cards, squircle containers, bottom sheets, and catalog tiles. Surfaces sit gently extruded above the warm canvas.
- **Text Layers:** Deep slate `#1E293B` provides strict WCAG AAA contrast for body and currency numbers against white and oat surfaces. `#64748B` handles structural metadata and table headers.

### Feedback
- **Status Success (`#16A34A`):** Hardware connection status (e.g., thermal printer connected), live synced state.
- **Status Alert (`#DC2626`):** Low inventory alerts, out-of-stock items, clock-out alerts.

## Typography

The typography pairs the friendly geometric curves of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the monospaced, high-precision clarity of **JetBrains Mono**.

### Plus Jakarta Sans (Brand, Headlines & Structural Copy)
Plus Jakarta Sans reinforces the soft, approachable aesthetic of the squircle surfaces. Headlines use weights `700` and `800` with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor promotional headers, modal titles, and category blocks. Body copy leverages medium weights (`500` and `400`) to guarantee high legibility on lower-resolution POS hardware and budget tablet displays.

### JetBrains Mono (Financial & Tabular Numerals)
All numerical metrics—Rupiah / currency amounts, item quantities, barcode SKUs, thermal receipt breakdowns, transaction tallies, and inventory stock figures—strictly utilize JetBrains Mono (`tabular-nums` formatting). Monospaced tabular alignment ensures decimal points and column sums align without jittering during live tally updates.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an 8pt base grid system adapted for ergonomic touch points, rapid scanning, and thumb-driven mobile ergonomics.

### Screen Adaptations & Layout Models
- **Mobile POS & Merchant App (<768px):** Single-column vertical flow with a bottom-anchored action bar. Touch targets are a minimum of 48×48px. Uses fixed margins of `1rem` and compact item gaps (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to maximize vertical space.
- **Tablet Cashier Split Layout (768px – 1024px):** Ergonomic 2-Column Split POS layout.
  - **Left Pane (60% width):** Scrollable catalog grid (3 columns) with top category pill tabs and instant barcode search.
  - **Right Pane (40% width):** Fixed receipt/order staging tray with live subtotal calculation, tender shortcuts, and a prominent checkout button.
- **Desktop Mini-ERP (>1024px):** 12-column layout with a compact left collapsible sidebar (Deep Emerald Teal tone), multi-card KPI dashboards (4 columns across), and dense inventory data tables with sticky summary headers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tactile Claymorphic Soft-UI** rather than standard flat surfaces or heavy drop shadows. Surfaces mimic softly extruded white clay sitting atop warm porcelain bases.

### The Claymorphism Stack
Every surface card and squircle container utilizes a triple-layer shadow formula combining warm tinted occlusion with ambient diffusion:
1. **Highlight / Inner Bevel:** A subtle top-left white edge reflection (`inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`).
2. **Contact Shadow:** A tight, low-distance warm tone shadow (`0 2px 4px 0 rgba(110, 60, 30, 0.04)`).
3. **Ambient Clay Diffusion:** A wide, soft, caramel-tinted blur (`0 10px 24px -4px rgba(140, 75, 40, 0.07)`).

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#FAF5EE` background, no shadows.
- **Level 1 (Extruded Cards & Squircles):** Product catalog cards, input containers, bottom app bars. Uses the soft claymorphic shadow stack with `12px` to `20px` blur.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Controls):** Pill action buttons, floating cart counts, and active category pills. Uses a terracotta ambient aura (`0 8px 18px -2px rgba(184, 78, 41, 0.28)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Absensi clock-in gates and tender payment dialogs. Sits on a 40% warm frosted backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.35)`), with deep soft-diffuse framing (`0 24px 48px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.18)`).
- **Decorative Watermark:** The official RASH geometric mandala is placed as a subtle background accent in hero headers and modal backplates at `5%` to `8%` opacity in Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary revolves around **squircles** and **pill geometry**, evoking the smooth, tactile feel of molded clay pebbles.

### Corner Radius System
- **Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Applied to all interactive primary CTA buttons ("Order Now", "Join Now", "Bayar"), counter increment/decrement capsules, floating badges, and active filter tabs.
- **Smooth Squircles (`rounded-2xl` / 16px - 20px):** The default for all content surfaces: product catalog cards, order summary containers, modal dialogs, and promotion banners.
- **Micro Squircles (`rounded-xl` / 12px):** Applied to form fields, search bars, category item icons, and receipt row items.

### Decorative Accents
- **Curved Wave Silhouettes:** Promotional hero banners feature a smooth organic curved wave mask, referencing the soft ribbon contours of the brand aesthetic.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pill CTA):** Filled with Burnt Terracotta (`#B84E29`), pure white typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans 600`), pill shape (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 28px`. Elevated by a terracotta glow shadow (`0 8px 20px rgba(184, 78, 41, 0.24)`). On tap/hover, darkens smoothly to `#943A1B` with a slight physical depression (`transform: translateY(1px)`).
- **Secondary Action (Warm Ivory Pill):** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 130, 43, 0.2)`, text Burnt Terracotta or Caramel Gold.
- **Cart/Add Button (+ Pill):** Compact 36×36px squircle/circle pill in Burnt Terracotta with an inset white plus icon, providing instant tactile satisfaction on item addition.

### 2. Cards (Product & Metric Squircles)
- **Catalog Product Card:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` body with `rounded-2xl` corners, subtle warm clay shadow. Imagery sits in an unbordered upper frame with rounded corners. The lower content area displays the item title, tabular pricing (`JetBrains Mono`), and a terracotta quick-add button.
- **Owner Dashboard KPI Card:** Pure white squircle featuring a delicate 2px top accent border in Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`) or Deep Emerald Teal (`#0A4D4D`). Emphasizes the main metric in tabular numbers alongside percentage badges.

### 3. Navigation
- **Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar:** Floating squircle dock (`rounded-full` or `rounded-3xl`) suspended 16px above the viewport bottom. Surface `#FFFFFF` with clay ambient shadow. Active icons are encased in an elevated Burnt Terracotta pill, while inactive icons render in Slate `#94A3B8`.
- **Top Header Bar:** Clean warm oat milk background with brand typography or emblem, search capsule, hardware printer status icon, and customer profile avatar.

### 4. Chips & Category Filters
- **Active Category Chip:** Pill-shaped with a solid Terracotta or Caramel fill, bold white text, and an ambient warm shadow.
- **Inactive Category Chip:** Pure white squircle or pill with subtle border (`1px solid #EFE8DC`), text `#64748B`.
- **Mini Badges:** Small squircle capsules (`padding: 2px 8px`) indicating "Hot", "Promo", or "Stok Rendah" using tinted fills (`#FBEFEA` with `#B84E29` text).

### 5. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Search Capsule:** Pill-shaped `#FFFFFF` surface with an embedded left search icon, inset text placeholder in `#94A3B8`, and clean `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` border. Focus state activates a subtle 2px ring in Caramel Gold (`#D9822B`) with a clay shadow glow.

### 6. Hardware & Status Indicators
- **Thermal Printer Icon:** Compact status pill located in the header. Features a miniature printer glyph beside a dynamic pulse indicator: glowing `#16A34A` when Bluetooth is connected and `#DC2626` when disconnected or paper is empty.

### 7. Modals & Clock-In Gate
- **Absensi Clock-in Screen:** Centered squircle card framed with a delicate gold accent border (`#C5A059`), displaying the RASH brand mark with the geometric watermark radiating behind it. Locks operational POS actions until the cashier PIN or biometric confirmation is registered.
