## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Modern Luxury Fintech** with **High-Density Operational POS Utility**. It is engineered specifically for MSME proprietors, inventory controllers, and front-desk billing operators who handle long operational shifts yet require the authority, trust, and refinement of enterprise financial tooling.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Drivers
- **Executive Precision:** Elevates routine retail and wholesale billing into a prestigious, high-accuracy financial experience.
- **Calm Resilience:** Relieves cognitive fatigue during sustained 8–10 hour retail shifts via warm, non-glare surfaces and disciplined contrast.
- **Tactile Confidence:** Every action—from barcode scans to settlement tenders—provides instant, clear feedback to prevent duplicate transactions and terminal lag.

### Design Aesthetic: Luxury Tactile POS
The aesthetic marries the clarity of modern transactional interfaces with rich, heritage fintech cues:
- **Tonal Anchor:** Deep Oceanic Teal provides weight, institutional trust, and stability across sidebars, primary actions, and primary ledger balances.
- **Accented Prestige:** Champagne Gold is deployed selectively for transactional culminations: final payable amounts, cash drawers, settlement confirmations, and loyalty designations.
- **Ergonomic Density:** Balances dense data tables (inventory stocks, multi-tax ledgers, batch serials) with spacious touch targets (minimum 44px) tailored for dual touch-screen POS and desktop mouse/barcode setups.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional stability with operational usability under indoor fluorescent and variable retail lighting.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#003B4B`) & Primary Variant (`#095368`):** Deep Oceanic Teal serves as the system anchor. Used for primary navigation frames, master action buttons, invoice status headers, and ledger titles.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`) & Secondary Variant (`#C5A059`):** Champagne Gold functions as the transactional accent. Reserved for grand totals, active cash tender badges, settlement CTA outlines, high-tier customer indicators, and active payment pill highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9F9F7`):** Warm Pearl Ivory replaces clinical digital white across root application viewports, reducing eye strain and glare during prolonged billing shifts.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure White crisp containers dedicated to receipt previews, active cart lines, modular cards, and modal sheets.
- **Structural Lines (`#E2E8F0`):** Soft slate gray boundary line separating register lanes, data rows, and input enclosures.

### Functional Operational Tokens
- **Online / Active Sync:** `#059669` (Deep Mint Emerald)
- **Offline / Stored Local:** `#D97706` (Warm Amber)
- **Terminal Critical / Disconnect:** `#DC2626` (Crimson Carmine)
- **Hardware Active (Scanner / Printer / Drawer):** `#0284C7` (Cobalt Blue)

## Typography

The typographic strategy relies on a dual-engine structure designed for rapid information retrieval, numerical safety, and premium brand balance:

### Primary Engine: Plus Jakarta Sans
Provides humanist geometry, tall x-heights, and clear aperture definitions. Used across navigation menus, modal titles, customer identifiers, vertical category tabs, action items, and general body copy.

### Secondary Numeric Engine: JetBrains Mono
Deploys fixed-width tabular characters across all financial and machine data:
- **Currency & Itemized Totals:** Eliminates horizontal layout jitter during rapid multi-item ring-ins.
- **Inventory Codes & Barcodes:** Guarantees unambiguous recognition of `0` vs `O`, `1` vs `l`, and special delimiter symbols.
- **Shift Clocks & Live Timers:** Keeps elapsed shift duration and queue speeds aligned.
- **Micro Labels:** `label-caps` in JetBrains Mono delivers crisp readability on thermal receipt preview mocks and hardware status indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around the **Split-Screen Transaction Architecture**, tailored for fast retail lines, wholesale dispatch, and ERP management.

### Responsive Form Factors & Grid Logic
- **POS Dual-Split Viewport (Desktop / 10"–15" Tablets, ≥1024px):**
  - **Left Section (60% width):** Product catalog, dynamic search, category clusters, and high-density inventory tables.
  - **Right Section (40% width):** Persistent transaction docket, itemized pricing lines, live customer selector, and final pay tender trigger.
  - Uses fixed margin gutters (`gutter-desktop: 1.5rem`) to maximize screen area for item listing.
- **Mobile POS & Stocktaking Viewport (<768px):**
  - Single-column flow transitioning into a docked bottom transaction bar.
  - Cart access transitions into an ergonomic slide-over sheet.
  - Touch targets expand to meet a minimum vertical boundary of 48px.

### Operational Spacing Guardrails
- Core interactive gap rhythm relies on `space-sm` (8px) for related control groups (e.g., quantity steppers, discount pills).
- Section separations use `space-lg` (16px) and `space-xl` (24px) to prevent accidental mis-taps during high-throughput checkout workflows.

## Elevation & Depth

This system departs from heavy material drop shadows, instead using **ambient structural depth** with subtle tinted diffusions to support long shift readability.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat Warm Pearl Ivory (`#F9F9F7`). Grounding layer with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Operational Cards & Grid Tiles):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px baseline border of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow is an ambient teal-tinted whisper: `0px 2px 6px rgba(0, 59, 75, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Cart Dock & Quick-Key Panels):** Raised white surface bordered with `#E2E8F0` and accompanied by a gold-teal hybrid drop: `0px 6px 16px -2px rgba(0, 59, 75, 0.06), 0px 0px 0px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Tender Trays, & Cash Drawers):** High-focus floating layer supported by a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with a deep containment shadow: `0px 16px 36px -4px rgba(0, 59, 75, 0.16)`.

### Tactile Feedback
Interactive buttons and catalog items feature an instantaneous inset elevation change upon actuation (`active` state: `transform: scale(0.98)` with an inner contact shadow `inset 0px 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`), providing immediate touch confirmation.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a unified **12px (`0.75rem` / `roundedness: 2`) curvature** across major structural cards, catalog product tiles, input frames, and modal dialogs.

### Shape Expression
- **Standard Structural Elements:** 12px corners convey high-end consumer hardware polish while maintaining the disciplined silhouette of an enterprise application.
- **Micro-Controls (Chips, Badges, Status Dots):** Retain a soft-pill form (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for scanning indicators, hardware readiness markers, and tier tags, visually distinguishing operational states from interactive cards.
- **Touch Bounds:** All interactive touch nodes ensure an absolute minimum clickable hit box of 44px × 44px, rounding out input fields cleanly without sacrificing tap precision.

## Components

### Buttons & Tender Triggers
- **Primary Operational Button:** Solid Deep Oceanic Teal (`#003B4B`), pure white typography, 12px border-radius, minimum 48px height. On hover/focus: transitions to `#095368`.
- **Settlement & Checkout CTA:** Deep Teal fill framed with a 1.5px solid Champagne Gold border (`#D4AF37`) or luxury Gold background with Teal bold typography for final invoice capture.
- **Secondary / Action Icons:** Crisp white container with 1px `#E2E8F0` border, charcoal text, and instantaneous teal wash on hover.

### Operational Hardware & Connectivity Badges
- Compact pill elements using `label-caps` in JetBrains Mono font.
- Composed of an 8px illuminated ping dot adjacent to status copy:
  - *Thermal Printer Online:* Soft mint fill (`#ECFDF5`), deep emerald text (`#065F46`).
  - *Bluetooth Barcode Scanner Linked:* Soft sky fill (`#F0F9FF`), rich cobalt text (`#075985`).
  - *Cloud Sync Offline / Buffer Mode:* Soft amber fill (`#FFFBEB`), warm amber text (`#92400E`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Pure white background, 44px height for standard data input, 52px for master barcode/SKU omnibox.
- 1px neutral border (`#E2E8F0`). On focus, shifts to a 1.5px solid Deep Teal (`#003B4B`) with a 3px soft gold glow ring (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.20)`).
- Leading icons for barcode scanning and clear buttons for rapid resets.

### High-Density Data Tables (Stock, ERP Ledgers)
- Compact row height (40px–48px) to maximize on-screen records.
- Alternating subtle rows (`#FFFFFF` to `#FAF9F6`).
- JetBrains Mono for all SKU numbers, stock quantity balances, unit costs, and GST percentages.
- Persistent right-aligned numerical data for easy column scanning.

### Product Catalog Tiles (POS Grid)
- Structured 12px rounded cards with high-contrast text.
- JetBrains Mono price tags anchored at the bottom-right inside a champagne-tinted tag.
- Visual touch-feedback ripple with thumbnail preview and real-time inventory counter.

### Slide-Over Cart & Receipt Panel
- Persistent split container using a pure white background separated by a distinct left border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Line-item quantity steppers configured with 36px touch targets.
- Bottom summary card featuring subtotal, discounts, tax breakdowns, and a prominent Champagne Gold-highlighted grand total.
